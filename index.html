High Reliability Architecture (Offline-First)

This software is designed with an Offline-First architecture. All your master records, dispatch entries, contractor assignments, and daily style sheets are instantly saved in your local browser cache. You can use the software with 100% full speed even without an internet connection, and whenever you connect to Neon PostgreSQL, all your data synchronizes to the cloud!
